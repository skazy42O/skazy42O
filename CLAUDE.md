# Car edit preferences (skazy42O)

- **Into-the-wheel transition ("kroz felnu") – use it in almost every future car edit.**
  On a beat, hold (freeze) a frame where the whole wheel is steady, with a hand-traced spoke-gap mask for that frame.
  The NEXT shot (preferably the exhaust backfiring) is visible *through the gaps between the spokes*; the camera dives
  into a gap with zoom blur while the spokes fly past, and the next shot (slower zoom = parallax, slightly darker until
  through) fills the screen right as the exhaust pops. No graphic rings/overlays – it must look clean and physical.
  SFX: reverse-whoosh into the wheel + swoosh. Reference: `wheelgap_transition()` in `urus-edit/scripts/render_urus.py`
  (mask: `urus-edit/scripts/wheel_gap_crop.npy`). The earlier glowing-ring portal version was rejected by the user.
- Cuts and transitions locked to the song's beat grid; drop = car launches.
- Real exhaust recordings (sathyaram/exhaustnotes) + backfire flames tracked on the exhaust; editor SFX from crafter-station/elements (CC0).
- Deliver the full render on GitHub (branch), no preview needed unless asked. Talk to the user in Serbian.
