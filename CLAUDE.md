# Car edit preferences (skazy42O)

- **Through-the-wheel transition ("kroz felnu") – use it in almost every future car edit.**
  Find a moment where the whole wheel is steady in frame, track the hub (spoke centre), and on a beat:
  the camera dives into the hub while the wheel spins up (rotational blur), the hub slides to screen centre,
  and the next shot opens from the hub through a growing circular portal with a glowing teal/white rim lip,
  counter-spinning to rest. SFX: reverse-whoosh into the hub + swoosh as the portal opens.
  Reference implementation: `wheel_transition()` / `HubTracker` in `urus-edit/scripts/render_urus.py`.
- Cuts and transitions locked to the song's beat grid; drop = car launches.
- Real exhaust recordings (sathyaram/exhaustnotes) + backfire flames tracked on the exhaust; editor SFX from crafter-station/elements (CC0).
- Deliver the full render on GitHub (branch), no preview needed unless asked. Talk to the user in Serbian.
